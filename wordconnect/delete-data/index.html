Delete Your Data

Word Connect by WordFolio · Grafton Pixel Studio · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: Word Connect has no accounts, and your progress is stored only on your device. You can delete it yourself at any time, or email us and we will delete anything we hold about you.

Request deletion

  1. Email graftonpixelstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Word Connect data deletion".
  2. Tell us which device you play on (Android or iPhone). If you want data held by our advertising partners removed too, include your device's advertising ID if you can (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  3. We confirm the request comes from you, delete the data and reply within 30 days.

Delete it yourself

  • Game progress (levels completed, coins and other items, settings): uninstall the game, or on Android clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Word Connect → Storage → Clear storage). This deletes it immediately; we have no copy.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, reset or delete it (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID); on iPhone, turn off tracking (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Advertising partners can then no longer link past ad data to your device.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your emails with us and anything else we hold about you, and, if you include your advertising ID, we pass your request on to our advertising partners (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads) so they can delete the data linked to it.
  • Kept: purchase records held by Google Play or the Apple App Store, which they keep under their own policies; we cannot delete these. Items such as No Ads can still be restored through the store.
  • Our advertising partners keep data under their own retention rules; see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which also explains how to ask Unity directly.
