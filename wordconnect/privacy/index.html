Privacy Policy

Word Connect by WordFolio · Grafton Pixel Studio · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: you can play without an account, and we don't ask for your name or email. Your progress is saved on your device. Our advertising partners use your device's advertising ID to show ads. If you choose to sign in, we use your account to back up and sync your progress, and you can delete it at any time.

This policy explains what information Word Connect by WordFolio (the "game") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. "We" means Grafton Pixel Studio, the game's developer, which is responsible for the information described here.

1. Playing without an account

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or any account to play.
  • Your game progress (levels completed, coins and other items, settings) is stored on your device. Uninstalling the game removes it, unless you have signed in and backed it up (section 2).

2. Optional sign-in and cloud backup

You can choose to sign in, for example with your Google or Apple account, to back up your progress and use it on more than one device. If you do:

  • We receive an account identifier and, if the sign-in provider shares it, your email address.
  • We store your game progress (levels completed, items, settings) with our cloud service provider so it can be restored and synced.
  • We use this only to run sign-in, backup and sync, and to answer you if you contact us.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data at any time from the game's settings, or by emailing us (section 14). Deleting your account removes your backed-up progress from our servers.

Sign-in is optional; the game works fully without it.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity's ad mediation) and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and limit how often you see the same ad, these partners collect information from your device, which can include:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where you allow it);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps, completed video ads).

Ads may be personalised based on this information, depending on your device settings and, where required by law (for example in the EU and UK), your consent. Until we ask for that consent in the game, ads for players in those regions are not personalised.

These partners process the information under their own privacy policies: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (covers LevelPlay and Unity Ads).

Rewarded ads are always your choice. Buying "No Ads" removes the ads that play automatically; rewarded ads stay available if you want them.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive a confirmation of what was bought so we can deliver it, but we never see or store your payment details.

5. Crash reports and diagnostics

Google Play and Apple may provide us with crash reports and basic performance statistics, which do not identify you, to help us fix problems.

6. Sharing

  • We share information only with the service providers described above (advertising partners, our cloud service provider, Google and Apple), only for the purposes described, or when required by law.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Some laws treat sharing data with advertising partners for personalised ads as "sharing"; you can opt out as described in sections 10 and 11.

7. International transfers

We and our partners, such as Unity, Google and Apple, may process information in countries other than yours. Where the law requires it (for example for players in the EU or UK), these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, as used by those partners.

8. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Keeping and deleting information

  • Information on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Account and cloud backup data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete your account.
  • To request deletion or a copy of information we hold about you, email us at graftonpixelstudio@gmail.com.

10. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. On iPhone, you can refuse tracking when the game asks, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalised ads: where the game asks for consent, you can decline or change your choice later.
  • Account: you can play without signing in, and delete your account at any time.

11. Your rights

EU, UK and similar laws. You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, to receive a copy of it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent (for example for personalised ads). We use your information to provide the game you asked for (sign-in, backup), on the basis of your consent (personalised ads where consent is required), and for our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the game (for example non-personalised ads and crash reports). You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

US state laws (for example California, Virginia, Colorado). You can ask to know, access, correct or delete your personal information, and to opt out of "sharing" or "targeted advertising", which covers personalised ads. To opt out, turn off or reset your advertising ID (section 10) or contact us. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To use any of these rights, email us at graftonpixelstudio@gmail.com. We may need to confirm the request comes from you, and we will reply within the time the law requires.

12. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, and our partners handle it under their own security practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game's features or partners change, and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the game or on its store page.
