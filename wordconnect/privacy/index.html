Privacy Policy

Word Connect by WordFolio · Grafton Pixel Studio · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: you can play without an account, and your progress is saved on your device. Our advertising partners collect device information, including your advertising ID, to show ads.

This policy explains what information Word Connect by WordFolio (the "game") collects and shares. "We" means Grafton Pixel Studio, the game's developer.

1. Your game data

We do not ask for your name, email address or an account. Your game progress (levels, coins and other items, settings) is stored on your device.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, these partners collect information from your device, which can include:

  • your device's advertising ID and other identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app performance);
  • how you interact with ads and the game, which can include in-app purchases.

Ads may be personalised, depending on your device settings and, where the law requires it, your consent. These partners handle this information under their own policies: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

4. Sharing

We share information only with the partners described above, or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information for money. Some laws treat sharing information with advertising partners for personalised ads as a "sale" or "sharing"; you can limit it as described in section 6.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad tracking in your device settings, which limits personalised ads.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, and to opt out of its "sale", "sharing" or use for targeted advertising. To make a request, email us (section 9). We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

7. Keeping and deleting

You can delete the game data on your device by uninstalling the game or clearing its data. To ask us to delete information, email us or see Delete Your Data.

8. Security and changes

Information the game sends to our advertising partners is encrypted in transit. We will update this policy when the game's features or partners change, and change the date at the top.
